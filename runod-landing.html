Our Beneficiary Mission: Project SILAW

Spearheaded by JCI Candon Gold Leaf, Project SILAW is funded by The RUNod Ayat 2025 as a community education initiative designed to help children in remote, mountainous areas transition away from heavy manual labor—such as making charcoal—and into formal schooling.

Proceeds from your registration provide crucial learning kits, school supplies, and community support so these children can dream bigger. In RUNod Ayat 2026, we are expanding this educational lifeline to reach even more mountain communities.
